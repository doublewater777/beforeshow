# 最想看 is a multi-select heart, not a star

Users mark 最想看曲目 with a **heart** control (not a star). Multiple songs may be marked at once. Marking is independent of user-added songs. On regenerate (after confirmation), every 最想看 song is preserved by song name + artist identity, same as hand-added preservation.

This replaces the interim “star / isStarred” product language. The trade-off is closer alignment to the home feature-cards prototype and clearer emotional wording versus reusing a generic star metaphor.
