# App-facing name is 歌单猜想

In-app user-visible language for this feature is **歌单猜想** and colloquial CTAs such as **猜一份歌单**. The older product term **候选曲目** is retired from App UI, tips, Pro copy, and empty states. Code identifiers may keep English names such as `CandidateSong` without exposing them to users.

App Store metadata, ASO keywords, and other external listing copy may still say 候选曲目 until a separate release/metadata pass; that lag must not block App-internal consistency. The trade-off is warmer product voice versus temporary dual language outside the app; we chose voice inside the product first.
