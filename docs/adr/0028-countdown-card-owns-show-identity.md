# Let the countdown card own current-show identity

The home screen keeps the complete 3:4 cover as a pure visual surface and moves current-show identity—status, show name, date, and venue summary—into the existing countdown card. This preserves the cover's visual scale and dynamic-cover behavior while giving lifecycle state and time one stable, accessible reading order; the card keeps its existing style, 20pt separation from the cover, and lifecycle-specific primary action.
