# Split App Store and atmospheric taglines

Search metadata and brand sentences are different jobs.

- App Store name: `开场前 - 演唱会倒计时`
- App Store subtitle: `音乐节与Livehouse倒数提醒`
- Description opening / in-product line: `开场之前，先进入状态`
- Splash, website hero, promotional text, Xiaohongshu titles: `灯亮之前，先进入状态`

The store name and subtitle exist to be indexed. The two brand sentences exist to convert and stay in memory. Do not put the brand sentence back in the 30-character subtitle — it wastes the second-highest search field on words nobody types.

Supersedes the 2026-06 decision that used `开场之前，先进入状态` as the App Store subtitle. Keyword research on 2026-08-19 showed `演唱会倒计时` / `音乐节` / `Livehouse` are the words people actually search.
