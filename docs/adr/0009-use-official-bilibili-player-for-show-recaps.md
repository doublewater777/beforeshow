# Use the official Bilibili player for show recaps

BeforeShow V2.1 may show Bilibili-based show recaps through Bilibili's official external player or by opening the original Bilibili page. The app will not cache covers, videos, audio, comments, danmaku, or source pages, and will not parse streams or build its own player; this keeps the feature focused on helping users preview live atmosphere without redistributing third-party media.
