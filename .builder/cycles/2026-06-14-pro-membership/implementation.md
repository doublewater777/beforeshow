# Pro Membership Implementation

Planned scope:

- Add Pro membership to Settings.
- Put Pro membership first in Settings, with current status, upgrade, restore purchases, benefits, and allowance explanation.
- Free users can save one show.
- Free users receive one device-local first-experience package: one candidate song generation, one outbound draft, one return draft, and one show recap generation.
- Gate repeated candidate song generation, repeated outbound/return draft generation, additional show recap discovery, and unlimited saved shows behind Pro.
- Offer Pro through App Store subscriptions at ¥12/month and ¥68/year, without a free trial.
- Use App Store purchase restoration without a BeforeShow account.
- Keep all long-term show data local-first.
- Keep show fragments, show preparation, tonight's song, and manual editing free.

Non-goals:

- Public community monetization.
- Ticketing or transaction take rate.
- Android billing.
- BeforeShow account, sync, or strong anti-abuse controls for uninstall/reinstall allowance resets.
