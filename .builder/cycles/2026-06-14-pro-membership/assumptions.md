# Pro Membership Assumptions

- Users perceive AI generation and unlimited saved shows as a paid convenience, not a tax on the emotional core.
- Limiting free usage can create a clear upgrade moment without blocking the first successful show experience.
- Paid users will have more than one show or stronger preparation intent, making recurring value plausible.
- Pro does not reduce the lightweight feeling of entering the show mood.

Falsified if users abandon setup when seeing limits, only pay once and disappear, or pay for non-core reasons that do not increase preparation behavior.
