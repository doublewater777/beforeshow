# Pro Membership Goal

BeforeShow Pro tests whether users with upcoming music shows will pay for repeated AI-assisted preparation and multi-show ownership. The target user is a music live-goer who has at least one real upcoming show and wants candidate song ordering, round-trip planning, show recaps, and unlimited saved shows without turning the app into a generic calendar or media manager.

Success means paid users use core preparation behaviors more than free users: adding more real shows, generating candidate songs, creating round-trip plans, and adding show fragments.

Out of scope: marketplace features, ticketing, account-based social features, public community, and Android monetization.
