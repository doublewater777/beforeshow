# Assumptions

- Users trust an explicit curtain-time confirmation more than a duration estimate.
- A compact, persistent shortcut row is easier to discover without reviving the retired full travel/memory product surface.
- Existing show fields are sufficient for lightweight actions: seat information for ticket context, venue/address for Apple Maps, app notification settings for reminders, and the system share sheet for companions.
- Existing current-show selection, detail navigation, add-show flow, and notification synchronization remain the correct seams.
- Two upcoming summaries provide enough orientation on Current; the complete library belongs in a separate tool-like destination.

Riskiest assumption: adding two compact later-show rows improves orientation without diluting the single-current-show focus.

Falsification: users treat the two-row summary as the complete library, or the secondary management page competes confusingly with the unchanged “我的现场” tab.

Existing evidence: direct user feedback requested that the shortcut row remain available at every phase. The actions stay lightweight and reuse current app/platform capabilities.
