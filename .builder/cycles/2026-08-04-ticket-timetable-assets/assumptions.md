# Assumptions

- A show-bound shortcut is easier to find than a separate ticket or utility area.
- Users want a visual reference during preparation and at the venue, but do not expect BeforeShow to replace the official ticketing app.
- One image per kind is enough for the first increment; replacement is more useful than an unbounded collection.
- Keeping the assets local and outside Companion sharing is an important trust boundary because ticket images can contain identifying information and QR codes.

The idea should loop back if users need multiple tickets, expect official validation, or interpret the local-only copy as a guaranteed no-backup/no-sync promise that the implementation cannot support.
