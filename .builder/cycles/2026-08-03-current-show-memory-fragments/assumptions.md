# Assumptions

- A compact shortcut on Current is discoverable enough without adding a new top-level tab.
- Private text and media capture strengthens the emotional value of a current show without turning BeforeShow into a social product.
- Three equal-width shortcuts remain legible on iPhone 17 when Memory Fragments includes a small count/status subtitle.
- Users accept that fragments from a former current show remain local but are only visible again if that show becomes current in this release.
- The idea is falsified if capture feels heavier than using Photos or Notes, or users interpret temporarily unavailable former-show fragments as data loss.

The product owner explicitly reversed the July retirement decision for this bounded, current-show-only version. The supplied product specification and the existing Current management surface are the design inputs.
