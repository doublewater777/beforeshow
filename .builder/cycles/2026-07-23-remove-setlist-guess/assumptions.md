# Assumptions

- A focused current-show countdown is more valuable than a broad collection of speculative AI tools.
- Setlist guessing is not required for users to understand or return to the current show.
- Because the app has not launched, deleting candidate-song local data models is safer than carrying compatibility code.

Riskiest assumption: removing the only remaining home feature card will make the product clearer rather than make the home screen feel incomplete.

Falsification: target users consistently ask for likely-song preparation or show lower return intent without it.

Existing evidence: the product owner explicitly chose to remove the feature after reviewing the current countdown-led experience.
