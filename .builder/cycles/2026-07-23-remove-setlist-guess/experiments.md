# Experiments

Test the simplified build with target users who have a real upcoming show.

Measure:

- Whether users can explain the app's purpose after seeing the current-show screen.
- Whether they understand the countdown state without prompting.
- Whether they ask where setlist prediction went or name it as missing value.

Pass threshold:

- The app has no setlist-guess surface or dead navigation path.
- In a small directional test, most users describe the app as a current-show/countdown companion without prompting.

Loop-back trigger:

- At least half of tested target users independently request likely-song preparation as a core need.
