# Retro

Belief: product focus improves when speculative AI features do not compete with the current-show countdown.

Learning: the feature was cross-cutting despite appearing as one card. Removing it required cleaning the persistence schema, current-show session, library/detail surfaces, notifications, Pro gates, privacy copy, backend transport usage, tests, and prototype assets together.

Decision: keep the iOS product centered on the current-show hero and countdown. Do not reintroduce song/setlist guessing without a new product cycle and fresh evidence.
