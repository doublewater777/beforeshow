# Goal

Retire memory fragments and travel planning so BeforeShow is a focused current-show countdown rather than a bundle of preparation and journaling tools.

Target user: a live-event attendee who wants one glanceable place for the next or current show.

Desired behavior: users open BeforeShow for the show identity, lifecycle state, and countdown without being asked to plan routes or create memories inside the app.

Success:

- No memory-fragment or travel-planning surface, model, permission, notification, generation endpoint, marketing promise, or active product requirement remains.
- The remaining app builds, tests, and launches on iPhone 17.

Out of scope: redesigning the surviving show, countdown, add-show, library, settings, and notification foundations.
